The Vision

CRAFTING DIGITAL SOULS

I'm miica, a solo 3D character artist dedicated to crafting high-fidelity characters for Unreal Engine and Unity. Every asset is crafted with full modular customization, realistic materials, and Apple Blendshapes — ready to drop into your project and make your own.

Guided by the Rabbit Heart spirit — a symbol of speed, care, and hidden depth — every character is designed not just to look good, but to feel alive in motion. Detail is the devotion.
